Overview

Bridging circuit design and embedded testing

As a member of UBC Rocket's avionics team, I contributed to two related areas of engineering work: hardware development and evaluation for a PYRO control board, and STM32-based peripheral configuration and interface validation.

The work combined schematic review, circuit simulation, microcontroller configuration, and practical debugging of hardware-software interfaces.

01 / Avionics Hardware

PYRO Board & Circuit Evaluation

  • Contributed to schematic design and hardware validation activities for the team's PYRO control board.
  • Reviewed electronic component datasheets to understand operating requirements and support circuit-level design decisions.
  • Used Altium Designer and LTspice for schematic work and circuit simulation.
02 / System Validation

STM32 Peripheral & Interface Testing

  • Configured STM32 peripheral interfaces and pin assignments in STM32CubeMX, including SPI, GPIO, and interrupts.
  • Performed firmware-level interface tests to examine microcontroller peripheral behavior.
  • Used UART output and logic-level checks to support debugging and hardware-software validation.
Engineering Process

From design review to observable behavior

These activities gave me experience connecting engineering decisions on the hardware side with peripheral-level behavior in embedded firmware.

01. Evaluate

Review component information and examine circuit behavior through schematic analysis and simulation.

02. Configure

Set up STM32 pins and peripheral interfaces with STM32CubeMX.

03. Validate

Use firmware tests, UART output, and logic-level checks to investigate interface behavior.

Key Takeaways

Hands-on experience with embedded peripheral configuration, board-level circuit analysis, practical interface debugging, and collaboration across hardware and firmware tasks.
